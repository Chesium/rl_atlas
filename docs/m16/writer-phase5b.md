## Added hypotheses and source discrepancies (for the mathematical reviewer)

1. **5.1/5.2 sign.** Printed (5.1) and (5.4) define violation by $>\lambda V$; the proofs use $q\le-\lambda V$. Lessons follow the proofs (as v1 did).
2. **5.1 gradient bounds as Lipschitz constants (added).** The proof uses $B_{\nabla V},B_{\nabla q}$ (suprema over $S$) along segments between points of $S$; the lesson takes them over a convex set containing $S$.
3. **5.1 strict inequality (added).** The C.1 $\delta$-argument claims $\mathbb B(\xi,r+\delta)\subseteq X$ for small $\delta$, false on the edge of $\widetilde X$; two-disk example where no good state lies within $r+\delta$. The lesson assumes $\nu(X_b)<\varepsilon$ (and notes the $\varepsilon'$ limit). It does not claim the conclusion itself fails.
4. **5.2 rate factor 2.** The proof gives $\preceq-(1-\eta)\lambda M$ (energy rate); C.2's final line and the theorem's wording claim $-2(1-\eta)\lambda M$.
5. **5.2 radius (new, consequential).** (a) Lemma C.4's bound $-\gamma(1-\epsilon c/2)$ should be $-(\gamma-\epsilon c/2)$, so the admissible block weight lacks the true rate; time-rescaling invariance supports the correction. (b) The condition-number bound $(L/m)^3B_H^2/(4(1-\zeta)^2)$ uses $\epsilon^2mI\preceq M'\preceq LI$, valid only for weight $\le1$. (c) Consequently the printed $r_b$ vanishes for linear $f$, and the bump-metric example satisfies the printed premises while its conclusion fails at $(0.514,0)$. The lesson keeps the printed statement, labels the failure, and gives a reconstruction $r_b^2=\sqrt2\,r(B_{\nabla q}+\lambda B_{\nabla V})\max\{\sqrt{L/m},(L/m)^{3/2}B_H/\lambda_\star\}/(\eta\lambda\mu)$ (with $\omega=1/2$), explicitly marked unaudited. The route contract's planned worked case ("linear $f$: Hessian-driven exclusion radius zero") is delivered as "the Hessian-driven part vanishes; the transfer part does not".
6. **5.2 cap measure.** Lemma C.2's incomplete-beta formula appears to omit Li's factor $\tfrac12$ and has "$r\ge1$" for "$r\ge\pi$"; the lesson uses the cap fraction named in the theorem.
7. **5.2 true-rate notation.** The paper uses one $\lambda$ for the true contraction and the learned decrease; the lesson separates $\lambda_\star$ (norm) and $\lambda$ (energy); the printed theorem is $\lambda_\star=\lambda$.
8. **Paper's VDP rate convention.** Algorithm 1 trains with energy rate $\lambda$; A.5 tests $\preceq-2\eta\lambda M$ (norm rate $\eta\lambda$).
9. **Scenario bound** (A.4.1, Calafiore) recalled with stated conditions only; not proved.
