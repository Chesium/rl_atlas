RL Atlas 1.1.0 adds a part on the tools that make continuous control work beyond the bare algorithms: exploration
noise, smooth policies and regularized critics, measured on the guide's own recorded runs.

**Download `rl_atlas.html` below and open it in your browser.** Nothing to install, no server, no account.

## New: Part 12, exploration and regularization

Eight stations between *Continuous control* and the *Toolbox*, each with a textbook chapter and a card. Five tell a
story from runs recorded over 20 seeds:

- **Action noise**, white, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck, pink or red: on continuous Mountain Car, where the car must swing back
  and forth to reach the flag, TD3 ends up reaching it on 1, 1, 18 and 7 seeds of 20.
- **Noise in the weights**: on the car, it ends up reaching the flag on 1 seed of 20 with raw observations, and on 15
  once they are normalized.
- **gSDE**, noise that depends on the state: SAC reaches the flag on no seed with its own noise, on all 20 with pink
  noise and on 12 with gSDE. Holding each of gSDE's noise matrices for 32 steps takes 9 seeds of 10 there, with a fifth
  of pink noise's jitter.
- **Gaussian policies in practice**: PPO on Pendulum from three starting spreads. The widest learns fastest, and jerks
  its torque the most.
- **Smooth policies**, CAPS, penalties and filters: TD3 holds Pendulum up either way, but without CAPS its torque comes
  to jump from step to step (a jitter of 16, against 1).
- Three more, without a story: **regularizing the critic** (weight decay, normalization layers, ensembles and dropout,
  many updates per step and the loss of plasticity, bigger critics), **robust policies** (from simulation to real
  robots: domain randomization, memory, teachers that see more, curricula) and **a recipe for continuous control**
  (what to try, in which order).

## Also new

- **Continuous Mountain Car**, a new world with its own view: the car in its valley, and a map of what the critic
  thinks of each position and speed, on which a successful episode spirals out to the flag.
- **Five new Lab experiments** (63 in all), racing the noises, CAPS and PPO's starting spreads, with odds over the
  noise's color and spread, how long gSDE holds each noise matrix, and CAPS's two weights.
- **New knobs** in the PPO lab on Pendulum (the starting spread, tanh squashing, normalized observations, gSDE) and in
  the DDPG lab (weight decay on the critic), with their odds. Like any setting, they train in the page. The PPO lab
  and the new ones chart the jitter: how much the action jumps from one step to the next.
- **Links into the new part** from DPG, DDPG, TD3, SAC, PPO, the toolbox and more; a reading path for continuous
  control on the Welcome page; the part's notation on the symbols page; hindsight experience replay in *Exploration
  strategies*.
- The *Toolbox* is now Part 13, and *Where next* Part 14.

## Fixed

- **Labs with several racers stutter less.** Their maps of what the network thinks are now drawn one path per color,
  as the film strip's have been since 1.0.3, and lose the faint grid they showed. In the headless checks here, six
  labs of two to four racers, each played at every speed, froze the page for over 50 ms 8 times instead of 48, the
  longest for 72 ms instead of 111.
